# Paired cross-system conformance protocol v1

Status: prospective protocol, dated 10 October 2026. This is a distinct study after the native/adapted development pilots and the PR37 finite-domain study; no earlier denominators are pooled with these results. `manifest.json`, `support.json`, and `corpus.json` are the machine-readable declarations.

## Question and units

Across three independent generation engines attempting identical explicit requests, which of generation, request binding, semantic score, and delivered MIDI boundaries pass? Compare the benchmark conditioning adapter for Constraint Music, music21's native figured-bass realizer, and Diatony's unchanged upstream Gecode model with a request-conditioning subclass and parameterized harness. Their internal rules and preferred registers differ. This tests shared-obligation conformance, not identical search problems or musical quality.

Development has four requests: C/G with I-IV-V-I or I-V-I. The primary holdout has twelve new request identities: D/A/E/F/Bb/Eb with I-ii-V-I (four chords) or I-vi-IV-ii-V-I (six chords). Held-out harmonic families and tonic keys are both absent from development. Tempos 90/108/132 cycle by key. Four additional requests probe unsupported minor/rest/tie/given-voice properties; all three systems record admission outcomes before generation. There are 12 development and 48 held-out attempt slots, of which 36 are primary. Unsupported rows are excluded from primary generation-failure denominators, but retained and reported separately.

## Contract and mapping

`contract.make_request` defines every required field. Admission requires exact field coverage and declared typed properties. Known out-of-domain obligations are UNSUPPORTED; malformed/unknown-field requests are BLOCKED. Degree indices are zero-based in the major scale. Each requested chord occupies four quarter notes in 4/4, with complete diatonic triad pitch classes and a root-position bass. Four SATB voices cover the full phrase continuously, without crossings (unisons allowed) or pitch changes inside a harmonic block. Durations are exactly 1 or 4 quarters and onset/duration values are canonical integer strings. Quarter-note attacks and whole-note attacks are both allowed by the request; they are preserved as distinct delivered events.

No pair of voices may move in the same nonzero direction between adjacent blocks while maintaining the same octave or fifth interval modulo 12. Every voice containing the major leading tone on V must move up exactly one semitone on the following I. The contract does not prohibit oblique perfect intervals or similar motion into a perfect interval from another interval. No additional aesthetic rule is inferred.

Constraint Music's benchmark subclass conditions its pinned compiled model with explicit degrees, inversion zero, stable voices and V-I obligations. Its native quarter-note output is untouched. Its native ranges, repetition and leap rules are recorded in native_input.json; the original objective is cleared to select first feasible output. Core source is unchanged and compared against the pinned baseline before collection.

music21 receives whole-note root basses at MIDI 48+root_pitch_class and original default figured-bass rules, augmented by a benchmark Segment subclass that posts the all-voice V-I leading-tone obligation during native possibility generation. The pinned internal movement DAG yields a lexicographically first viable complete path without enumerating every progression. Output is native chorale notation/MIDI with the request tempo explicitly inserted. This insertion and path-selection policy are disclosed adapter choices, not oracle-based repair.

Diatony receives the identical key, degree sequence and root inversions in an unchanged upstream model; a benchmark subclass posts complete triad presence and all-voice V-I leading-tone obligations in Gecode before search. The new C++ harness captures first-feasible DFS SATB pitches, writes the original native MIDI, and separately retains the captured score. `render.py` writes an explicitly adapted delivery from these captured events and the trusted request. Native and adapted MIDI are separate outcomes; native identity/context cannot silently count as passed.

## Budgets and capture

Run one fresh process at a time, rotate system order by request index, cap each process group at 30 seconds including startup/import/export, and set numerical-library thread environments to one. Constraint Music and Gecode additionally have 20-second native search limits; music21 lacks an equivalent native clock and is constrained by the outer cap. CP-SAT uses one worker and seed 7; Gecode is sequential DFS, music21 deterministic first-path selection. Timeouts kill the entire process group. Preserve request, actual score/input/MIDI bytes, normalized artifact, standard output/error and available worker metrics for every slot. Costs are parent wall time and maximum RSS of an individual worker/child process in Linux KiB, not summed concurrent pipeline RSS. No speed superiority claim is permitted.

## Independent checking

Native JSON/MusicXML/text captures are reconstructed independently of adapter normalization. Reconstructed events must equal normalized events, and captured native input must map to the trusted request. A direct pitch-class/rational-event semantic oracle checks score facts. A separate matrix-based implementation uses chord-quality intervals and integer-time occupancy; it imports neither oracle nor adapters. Any disagreement is BLOCKED, never majority-voted away. Hand-constructed positive and negative cases and metamorphic controls validate agreement during development.

A handwritten SMF parser and pinned mido parser independently reconstruct actual MIDI and must agree. SATB identities use tracks 1..4 with channels 0..3 for Constraint Music/adapted Diatony; music21 has tracks 1..4, channel 0 in each. Context must be exactly one key, meter, and tempo declaration at time zero: the requested major key, 4/4, and round(60,000,000/BPM) microseconds per quarter. Positive PPQN scaling with proportionally scaled ticks is allowed. Velocity, program and track names are ignored. Note pitch/onset/duration, voice identity, key/meter/tempo remain exact. No coalescing of repeated attacks is permitted. Malformed MIDI is BLOCKED; native Diatony identity/context is UNOBSERVABLE even when its unvoiced pitch/time relation can be measured.

Checks operate against an independently supplied trusted request; an embedded matching hash is insufficient. Unknown artifact fields, malformed scores or missing native captures cannot pass. Every record retains explicit request ID, request hash, evidence paths and byte hashes. End-to-end PASS requires request binding, native-score relation, semantic agreement/pass, integrity, parser agreement and exact delivered relation. Derive both the cumulative funnel and independent stage outcomes.

## Natural failures, faults and controls

Retain every planned attempt: OUTPUT, TIMEOUT, NO_SOLUTION, HARNESS_ERROR, UNSUPPORTED or BLOCKED. Retain natural semantic REJECT, parser BLOCKED and native UNOBSERVABLE separately. No retries, oracle-based selection, repair or silent substitution are allowed in held-out collection.

There are twelve deterministic fault slots per primary attempt, hence 432 planned slots: request key, request tempo, coordinated request+score+MIDI transpose, chromatic pitch, missing triad, crossing, score duration, MIDI pitch, MIDI duration, MIDI channel, MIDI tempo and truncated MIDI. Score faults repair event hashes and MIDI bytes to force fresh semantic checking; coordinated edits still must bind to the external request. Each witness must change score/request or actual bytes. All slots are eligible only on untouched, end-to-end accepted baselines. Other baselines retain INAPPLICABLE with BASELINE_NOT_ACCEPTED. Eligibility precedes observation; no missing-slot deletion or no-op witness is allowed. BLOCKED is distinct from REJECT, and either prevents acceptance without claiming a specific semantic diagnosis.

There are 36 planned positive PPQN slots, one per primary attempt with identical baseline eligibility. Eight independent hand-positive controls comprise two keys and two development families with original/scaled PPQN. All actual baseline and mutation bytes are archived (content-addressed mutation ZIP deduplicates identical bytes). Unique IDs, exact slot sets, archive member coverage, hashes, native reconstruction, both parsers, reference agreement and mutation replay are checked independently.

Analyze yield and cumulative request/artifact/delivery transitions with a fixed denominator of twelve primary requests per system. Report independent delivery pass, native full endpoint, all natural statuses, unsupported rows, fault eligibility/status/first boundary, known-valid false rejection and costs. Pair identical requests; mutation observations are correlated within baseline. Enumerate four ordered resamples of the two held-out harmonic-family clusters for paired end-to-end differences. The resulting ranges are illustrative, not population confidence intervals. The sample is purposive; no finite-data universal reliability or population generalization is claimed.

A completed machine-checkable bounded extension requires full paired collection and replay, semantic reference agreement, passing positive controls and no accepted injected fault. Natural baseline failure is a result, not a reason to change the contract. Report failures and limitations openly. A manuscript draft may integrate this study with PR37 as separate evidence. Broader novelty must be evaluated claim-by-claim against Pisters, Diatony, Harmoniser, Dai et al. and MusicConstraintBench; no global first claim is made.

## Permissions and limits

The owner authorized these runs. External projects retain their original licenses/notices; no upstream code or compiled binary is redistributed here. Constraint Music remains proprietary. The independent replay procedure supplies a case-by-case written permission request/grant template without creating an automatic public license. Outside replication and permission grants are not claimed completed. Minor, rests, ties, given voices and contextual chromatic harmony remain outside this shared contract; native capability must not be inferred from adapter support. Independent implementations and parsers can still share conceptual assumptions. Human listening, aesthetics, production reliability and optimization quality are outside the claim.
